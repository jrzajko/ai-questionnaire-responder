# Sub-processor List

## SUB-1 Current sub-processors
| Sub-processor | Purpose | Data location |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon Web Services | Cloud hosting, storage, backups | United Kingdom (London), EU (Ireland) |
| Northwind AI | Large language model inference for Acme Assist | EU |
| Vectorly | Text embedding model for Acme Assist search | EU |
| Postmark Mail Co. | Transactional email delivery | EU |
| HelpDeskly | Customer support ticketing | EU |

## SUB-2 Due diligence
Every sub-processor undergoes a security and privacy assessment before onboarding and is reviewed annually. All sub-processors are bound by a data processing agreement with obligations at least as protective as Acme's customer DPA.

## SUB-3 Changes
Acme gives customers at least 30 days' notice of any new sub-processor by email to subscribed contacts. Customers may object on reasonable data-protection grounds during the notice period.

## SUB-4 International transfers
Where a sub-processor processes personal data outside the UK or EEA, Acme relies on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and EU Standard Contractual Clauses. No current sub-processor processes customer content outside the UK or EEA.
