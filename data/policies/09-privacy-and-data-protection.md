# Privacy & Data Protection Statement

## PRV-1 Roles
For customer content, the customer is the data controller and Acme is the data processor. Acme processes customer personal data only on the customer's documented instructions, as set out in the Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

## PRV-2 Regulatory compliance
Acme's processing is designed to comply with UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and EU GDPR. Acme is registered with the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

## PRV-3 Data Protection Officer
Acme has appointed a Data Protection Officer, who can be contacted at privacy@acme-saas.example.

## PRV-4 Data subject requests
Acme provides tools that let customers export, correct and delete personal data to respond to data subject requests. If Acme receives a request directly, it forwards it to the customer without undue delay.

## PRV-5 DPIAs
Acme carries out data protection impact assessments for new processing likely to result in high risk, including every new AI feature. Acme will provide reasonable assistance to customers completing their own DPIAs.

## PRV-6 Records of processing
Acme maintains records of processing activities as required by Article 30 of the UK GDPR.

## PRV-7 Special category data
Acme Workspace is not designed for special category personal data. Customers are responsible for deciding whether to store such data in the platform.
